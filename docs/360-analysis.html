Layered on top: heavy AI capex compressing FCF conversion precisely while AI chat interfaces threaten to erode the query volume funding the entire TAC/ad-pricing model.
MEDIUMApple TAC channel concentration
~$20B+/year, ~75% of TAC spend, tied to a single counterparty relationship (Apple/Safari default placement) that remains subject to ongoing litigation risk even though near-term court decisions have preserved it. Any adverse future ruling is a direct hit to both search volume and TAC economics simultaneously.
