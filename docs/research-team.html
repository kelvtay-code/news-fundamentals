Evidence status of each gate

Each gate carries its measured status. A gate with no measured edge stays in the rules for consistency, not because a test supports it.

  • G1, G2, G3, G6 — unproven. The rules keep them. No test shows an edge.
  • G4 structure — measures backwards. The dropped group beat the kept group by +0.78%, t +1.82. G4 removed 10 of the 13 filtered names today, so treat that group as a measurement, not as avoided losses.
  • G5 conflict — unproven.
  • Wyckoff phase alone, and positive GEX — no edge. Do not read either one as support. 11 of 46 grid rows flag a GEX sign flip today, and the flip earns no score.
  • An up day with volume above 1.2x, and top-20% 20-day momentum — ANTI-PREDICTS, at -0.74%, t -2.9. Do not chase. TXN carries this condition today.
  • The 5-session pullback — the only validated edge. It buys the weakest 20% by 5-session return. The overlay pullback score is a different test, so it does not inherit that result.
  • A cushion under 2-3% — measured bad. 7 of 9 past spreads under 2% hit the exit rule, and a 2% cushion breaks about 2 times in 3. Rules v2 holds no minimum-cushion gate yet, so a thin trade stays rule-compliant.

Two trades cleared every gate today. That makes them rule-compliant, not proven.

How to read this

Gates run in order and the first failure decides. Figures in brackets are the win rate of that group in the 29/08–11/09 review (169 calls, 44% overall). G1 bull trades need fundamentals ≥ 7 [22%] · G2 a news score of 8+ without fundamentals ≥ 8.5, or a headline that already reports the move, means the news is in the price [12%] · G3 more than 8% in five days in the trade's direction goes to the watch list for a pullback [29%] · G4 no bull lean below the put wall or in Mark-down/Distribution [30% / 34%] · G5 conflicting direction, sentiment or phase is no trade, not a small one [39%] · G6 sentiment-only catch-alls and names with no technical read have no wall to sell against [28%].

Why credit spreads. Picking direction was close to a coin flip. Selling the wall only needs price to stay beyond it: in the review, bull calls held above the put wall 69% of the time against 48% for direction, and bear calls stayed below the call wall 85% against 58% (marked at the review date, not expiry). The catch-all still scans every bulletin ticker, but a 7+ now earns a watch-list line, not a trade.

Cushion is the distance from price to the short strike; THIN CUSHION means it is smaller than the 7-day implied move. Expiries default to the first Friday at or beyond 30 DTE (04 Dec, 30 DTE; confirm the expiry is listed on the chain) and move earlier when earnings fall inside it. Candidate generator, not a trade gate — confirm strikes, credit and size against your own risk limits.
