Sentilist read the actual headlines for each surviving ticker from the latest news bulletin and rated trade conviction 1-10 -- how real and specific the catalyst is, not just its tone. A strong, specific negative catalyst (a downgrade, a safety probe) scores as high as a strong positive one; the arrow (▲/▼) next to the score carries the direction, and the trade proposal follows the arrow, not just the score.

The 7+ catch-all is mandatory and independent of the funnel: it scanned all 794 headline cards across all 198 covered tickers using a catalyst-keyword pre-filter (earnings beats/misses, upgrades/ downgrades, FDA approvals, M&A, guidance changes, etc.), then read the ~51 flagged candidates directly to rate the 11 that were both genuinely ticker-specific and rated 7 or higher -- a heavy post-earnings-season news day (DELL, AVGO, HPE, PANW all reported) more than made up for the quiet Tier 1. This is a pre-filtered scan, not an exhaustive hand-read of all 198 tickers -- a real catalyst below the keyword net could exist but wasn't caught.

Conflicts are flagged, not hidden. Where Wyckoff phase, GEX regime, or wall position disagree with the news direction (SBUX, HON, GOOGL, GSK, AVGO, LLY, TXN), the trade is sized down or dropped to a watch-only call rather than forced -- see each card's amber conflict note.
