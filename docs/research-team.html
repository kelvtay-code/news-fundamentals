Sentilist read the actual headlines for each surviving ticker from the latest news bulletin and rated trade conviction 1-10 -- how real and specific the catalyst is, not just its tone. A strong, specific negative catalyst (a downgrade, a safety probe) scores as high as a strong positive one; the arrow (▲/▼) next to the score carries the direction, and the trade proposal follows the arrow, not just the score. Today is unusually top-heavy at the funnel stage -- 8 of 15 technicalist survivors cleared Sentilist ≥6, well above the typical 4-6 name range on prior run days.
