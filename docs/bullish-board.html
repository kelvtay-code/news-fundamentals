Bullish board — tradeable tiers

Read the tier first. Tier A carries two or more measured signals and no caution. Each tier counts one name for each correlation cluster: a second name in the same cluster goes to a "same bet" table below it. A thin cushion shows in red, and so does a credit below $0.10. A 2% cushion fails about two times in three across five sessions.

How to read the cushion and the credit. The cushion is the fall from spot down to the short strike, as a share of spot. The credit is a Black-Scholes model value, not a quote: price the spread on the screen before you trade it.
