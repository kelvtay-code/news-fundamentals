How to read this

Gates run in order and the first failure decides. Figures in brackets are the win rate of that group in the 29/08–11/09 review (169 calls, 44% overall). G1 bull trades need fundamentals ≥ 7 [22%] · G2 a news score of 8+ without fundamentals ≥ 8.5, or a headline that already reports the move, means the news is in the price [12%] · G3 more than 8% in five days in the trade's direction goes to the watch list for a pullback [29%] · G4 no bull lean below the put wall or in Mark-down/Distribution [30% / 34%] · G5 conflicting direction, sentiment or phase is no trade, not a small one [39%] · G6 sentiment-only catch-alls and names with no technical read have no wall to sell against [28%].

Why credit spreads. A direction call was close to a coin flip. A short strike at the wall only needs price to stay beyond it. In the review, bull calls held above the put wall 69% of the time, against 48% for direction alone. Bear calls stayed below the call wall 85% against 58%. The review marked these at the review date, not at expiry. The catch-all still scans every bulletin ticker, but a 7+ now earns a watch-list line, not a trade.

Cushion is the distance from price to the short strike; THIN CUSHION means it is smaller than the 7-day implied move. Expiries default to the first Friday at or beyond 30 DTE (11 Dec, 34 DTE; confirm the expiry is listed on the chain) and move earlier when earnings fall inside it.
