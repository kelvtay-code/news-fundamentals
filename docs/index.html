How to read this

Gates run in order and the first failure decides. The figures in brackets are that group’s win rate in the 29/08–11/09 review (169 calls, 44% overall). G1 bull trades need fundamentals ≥ 7 [22%] · G2 a news score of 8+ without fundamentals ≥ 8.5, or a headline that already reports the move, means the news sits in the price [12%] · G3 more than 8% in five days in the trade’s direction goes to the watch list for a pullback [29%] · G4 no bull lean below the put wall or in Mark-down or Distribution [30% / 34%] · G5 a conflict between direction, sentiment and phase is no trade, not a small one [39%] · G6 sentiment-only catch-alls and names with no technical read have no wall to sell against [28%].

The catch-all rule. The sentilist scans every ticker in the bulletin, not only the names the earlier stages carry forward. Any ticker it rates 7 or higher joins the report even with no fundamental or technical score. Under rules v2 those rows get a watch-list line, never a trade, because without a wall there is no level to anchor a short strike.

Why credit spreads are the default. A directional call was near a coin flip. A short strike at the wall only needs price to stay beyond that wall: in the review, bull calls held above the put wall 69% of the time against 48% for direction alone, and bear calls stayed below the call wall 85% against 58% (marked at the review date, not at expiry).

Cushion is the distance from price to the short strike. THIN CUSHION means the cushion is smaller than the 7-day implied move. Expiries default to the first Friday at or beyond 30 DTE (27 Nov 2026, 30 DTE — confirm the expiry is listed on the chain) and move earlier when earnings fall inside the window. If an earlier expiry leaves under 21 DTE, the name goes to the watch list instead. This report is a candidate generator, not a trade gate. Confirm strikes, credit and size against your own risk limits.
