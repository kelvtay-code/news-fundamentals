Gates run in order and the first failure decides. Figures in brackets are the win rate of that group in the 29/08–11/09 review (169 calls, 44% overall). Why credit spreads. Picking direction was close to a coin flip. Selling the wall only needs price to stay beyond it: in the review, bull calls held above the put wall 69% of the time against 48% for direction, and bear calls stayed below the call wall 85% against 58% (marked at the review date, not expiry). The catch-all still scans every bulletin ticker, but a 7+ now earns a watch-list line, not a trade.

Cushion is the distance from price to the short strike; THIN CUSHION means it is smaller than the 7-day implied move.
