Sentilist read the actual headlines for each surviving ticker from the latest news bulletin and rated trade conviction 1-10 -- how real and specific the catalyst is, not just its tone. A strong, specific negative catalyst (a downgrade, a safety probe) scores as high as a strong positive one; the arrow (▲/▼) next to the score carries the direction, and the trade proposal follows the arrow, not just the score.

The 7+ catch-all is mandatory and independent of the funnel: it scanned all 752 headline cards across all 192 covered tickers using a catalyst-keyword pre-filter (earnings beats/misses, upgrades/ downgrades, FDA approvals, M&A, guidance changes, etc.), then read the ~48 flagged candidates directly to rate the 10 that were both genuinely ticker-specific and rated 7 or higher. This is a pre-filtered scan, not an exhaustive hand-read of all 192 tickers -- a real catalyst below the keyword net could exist but wasn't caught.
