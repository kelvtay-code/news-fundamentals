For the ESPO position. China buys most of the ESPO and Sokol I am long. A market that loosens is also when my ESPO premium would be compressing anyway. I am noting it as a correlated risk, not adding a new trigger.

Method. Calls treat flat price, cracks, time spreads, cash differentials and freight separately, then test each against MOC price formation, completed deals and physical logistics. Twelve disciplines, each added after it cost money: (1) name the invalidation on the instrument actually traded; (2) a completed sale outranks the shape of the forward curve; (3) one session, or one lucky outcome, is not a signal; (4) every top-five position must cite a dated physical event, a completed realisation or a named counterparty; (5) a single dated event buys one notch, never two; (6) mark the print, not the memory; (7) invalidations use one sufficient condition or two joined by OR, never AND; (8) do not cut on the shape of the order book when the differential itself has not moved against you; (9) write the invalidation in the position’s own unit; (10) mark from the assessment table dated to the session, never from commentary, and a broker peg is not an assessment; (11) verify an asset was running before pricing its outage; (12) write triggers only on series that print every day in the source set.
