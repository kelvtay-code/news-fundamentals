⚠️ The thing worth interrogating
ALL CORRELATION
  • Seven of eight differentials rose on the same day every outright rose. In the previous eight sessions that has never happened once — the pattern has been outrights and differentials moving in opposite directions, which I have repeatedly described as the tell that a move is macro rather than physical.
  • Today they moved together, on a newspaper report. Kero +$4.08, HSFO +$4.08, LSFO +$1.67, gasoil +$1.15, gasoline +$0.92.
  • The single exception is naphtha: flat price +$28.63/mt, premium unchanged at $25.00. It is the only instrument whose structure refused to join in.
💡 When everything correlates to one, the information content of any individual differential falls. A $4.08 rise in the kero differential on the day of a war headline is worth much less as evidence than a $0.83 rise on a quiet day was — and the $0.83 rise on 30 September was the one that had a bid behind it. So I am reading today's differential moves as largely one observation, not seven, and the thing I actually take from the session is the exception: naphtha's premium did not follow, which is consistent with everything else the physical market said today.
