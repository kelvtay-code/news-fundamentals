💥 Houthi strikes on Aramco
NO FLOW IMPACT
  • Houthi spokesperson Yahya Saree, in a statement on X: "carried out a qualitative military operation targeting Aramco Company in Dhahran in the Eastern Province, King Khalid International Airport and the Aramco refinery in Riyadh".
  • He added that the operations would "continue in the coming days, striking the Saudi enemy's airports and oil facilities".
  • The Saudi Press Agency reported on 14 October that an Aramco administrative building in Dhahran was targeted on 13 October and caught fire. It cited an official energy ministry source. There were no injuries or fatalities.
  • Aramco did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
  • Houthi forces targeted Aramco in Riyadh on 3 October with ballistic missiles and drones.
  • Platts states the position plainly: "While the attack had no impact on oil production or flows, it exacerbated supply concerns and boosted prices."
💡 This is the fourth geopolitical event in three weeks that moved the price and did not move a barrel. Dhahran holds Aramco's headquarters, so the target carries symbolic weight. An administrative building fire is not a supply loss. My 8 October rule still applies. I do not build a position on a reported intention or on an attack without a measured volume behind it.
