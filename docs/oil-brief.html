Two-night strike pause (announced Jul 24, now Day 3-4) is the dominant driver of today's crash — but MOC/paper reaction has not been confirmed by physical flow data: Hormuz traffic unchanged at 16 ships Jul 25-26 (no VLCCs/LNG since Jul 12); Bab al-Mandab rose to 32 from 26 but stays below the 36 seen Jul 24.
