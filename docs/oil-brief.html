What it cost in price

  • European diesel gave back $32.00/mt and CIF ULSD $30.50/mt.
  • The bal-October gasoil EFS reversed $57.35 of Friday's $62.92 move.
  • The USGC gasoline A3 bid fell about two cents, from −9.25 to −11.00.

💡 I wrote on Friday that the asymmetry was "smaller than it looks" because "storm shut-ins restore in days", and that by publication the trade "will already be unwinding." That is exactly what happened, and it is worth stating why it was foreseeable rather than lucky: a shut-in has a published daily number and a known physical cause, so its reversal is observable in advance. A policy headline has neither. This is the second consecutive session in which the measurable item behaved predictably and the narrative item did not need to be guessed at.
