Discipline 7, added today, priced by the LSFO loss.

Write invalidations with OR, never AND. My Marine 0.5% stop read: “two consecutive sessions with no physical trade AND the bid stack below +$30.” Today a cargo cleared — at $6 below yesterday’s print, with the entire bid stack down four dollars to +$25/+$29. The meaningful half of my stop broke completely. The decorative half did not. Joined by AND, the stop could not fire, and a position that should have been cut on rule was cut on judgment instead.

A stop with two conditions joined by AND is not a stop, it is a wish. Each condition must be sufficient on its own, or it does not belong in the sentence. Every invalidation in Section 10 has been rewritten today to a single condition, or to two conditions joined by OR.

A second, smaller lesson, from the cracker short. I set that stop on Lotte’s Daesan and Yeosu units. The evidence that broke the thesis came from Hanwha Total’s Daesan crackers and Hyundai Oilbank’s Daesan RFCC — different companies, same town, running at 85% and 100%. A stop naming specific assets only covers those assets. Where a thesis is about a complex, the invalidation has to be about the complex.
