3  |  Geopolitics — What Moved, and What It Prices
Four developments carry price consequence today, and they do not point the same way. The tanker war escalated to direct US strikes on Iranian vessels; the strait simultaneously reopened to 14 transits and a second LNG cargo; OPEC+ froze quotas it is not filling anyway; and Russia opened a major new Arctic export system into a fleet that cannot lift it. The correct reading is not “bullish” — it is that the war premium is now concentrated in differentials and freight, while flat price is increasingly exposed to a peace headline.

Disciplines 5 and 6 were added on 7 September, after the four largest losses on the board shared a single cause and after a one-day date lag caused a 61-cent loss to be published as a 33-cent gain.
