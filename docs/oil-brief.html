  • 19 and 20 October are consecutive sessions with physical HSFO trades. My re-entry rule asks for exactly that, so the condition was satisfied at the 20 October close. I reported no trades on both days and kept the counter at 0 of 2.
  • One caveat I will not hide behind: the 19 October trade priced on a balance-October basis, not a MOPS cash differential. It sits in the physical trades section with a 40,000 tonne volume and a laycan, and my rule said "physical trades", not "MOPS-basis trades". By the rule as written it counts.
  • The cost: the differential was about +$46.33 at the 20 October close and is +$48.15 now. A rule-compliant entry would be roughly $1.82 in hand. I am not back-dating a position I did not take.

💡 This is the worst kind of error in this book: not a wrong opinion, but a pre-committed rule that fired and went unrecorded. The rule did its job and my reading of the data did not. The method is now fixed: every window's bids, offers and trades are read from that window's own labelled section, and I cross-check the "N trade(s)" counter against the explicit trade lines.
