What it means. Brent fell $3.53 on the possibility of a meeting. A meeting is not a ceasefire, and a ceasefire is not an escort mission. The escort mission is the thing that would actually normalise freight and insurance, and by the navy’s own account it comes last. My freight and propane positions are exposed to headlines this week, but not yet to their mechanism.

Method. Calls treat flat price, cracks, time spreads, cash differentials and freight separately, then test each against MOC price formation, completed deals and physical logistics; conflicting signals are never averaged. Thirteen disciplines, each added after it cost money: (1) name the invalidation on the instrument actually traded; (2) a completed sale outranks the shape of the forward curve; (3) one session, or one lucky outcome, is not a signal; (4) every top-five position must cite a dated physical event, a completed realisation or a named counterparty; (5) a single dated event buys one notch, never two; (6) mark the print, not the memory; (7) invalidations use one sufficient condition or two joined by OR, never AND; (8) do not cut on the shape of the order book when the differential itself has not moved against you; (9) write the invalidation in the position’s own unit; (10) mark from the assessment table dated to the session, never from commentary, and a broker peg is not an assessment; (11) verify an asset was running before pricing its outage; (12) write triggers only on series that print every day in the source set — breached for freight until today, now fixed; (13) when a measurement falsifies the premise a position was built on, cut size immediately and leave the stop to handle price.
