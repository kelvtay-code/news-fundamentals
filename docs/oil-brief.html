Method. Calls treat flat price, cracks, time spreads, cash differentials and freight separately, then test each against MOC price formation, completed deals and physical logistics; conflicting signals are never averaged. Every mark is read from a dated assessment table, never from a broker peg. Triggers are written only on series that print every day — and, from 24 September, only on series that demonstrate liquidity rather than merely a number. Where a position’s premise is falsified by a measurement, size is cut immediately rather than waiting for the price stop. Where an earlier edition is contradicted by later evidence, the correction is printed at the top, not buried.

Nothing here is investment advice. Conviction labels describe the strength of evidence in a single day’s assessment set, not forecasts or risk-adjusted return expectations.
