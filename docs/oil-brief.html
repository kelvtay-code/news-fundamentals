🟡 LPG / PROPANE — BULLISH (REUNIFIED FROM SPLIT CALL)
📈 Asia and Middle East legs rally together on the day's sharp crude jump
📊 Regional Price Signals

CFR North Asia LPG prices rose for a second straight session at the Aug 7 close, ahead of Singapore's long National Day weekend and amid stable demand — Platts assessed both CFR North Asia propane and butane cargoes $32/mt higher on the day at $701.50/mt and $726.50/mt respectively. CFR East China and CFR South China propane were assessed at $772/mt and $770/mt, both up $28/mt. "The sharp rise in Asian LPG prices was partly due to a $34/mt jump in Brent crude futures at the market close," Platts noted. On the Middle East side, FOB Arab Gulf propane and butane both rose too — propane +$10/mt to $564/mt, butane +$9/mt to $583/mt — reversing the prior two sessions' separate decline and now tracking the same crude-driven move as Asia. Middle East LPG prices rose "in line with the $34/mt day-on-day increase in front-month Brent crude oil futures."

📋 Tender Activity

During the end-of-day MOC, PetroChina's bid for 46,000 mt of propane for second-half-September loading (at balance-of-August FEI minus $1/mt, translating to $699/mt) was lifted by Vitol — a confirmed, genuine physical trade. The Asian propane-naphtha spread widened to minus $75/mt from minus $63/mt the previous session, propane weakening relative to naphtha even as both rallied in outright terms.

🏭 Logistics / Supply Signals
  • • Front-month FOB Saudi Arabia propane swaps fell $11/mt to $573/mt even as spot cash rose — a genuine near-term/spot divergence; second-month swaps rose $10/mt to $540/mt. The propane-butane FOB Saudi swap spread held near flat at minus $19/mt (from minus $20/mt), "in line with a trade reported done at that level."
  • • Persian Gulf-Japan VLGC freight fell to $206/mt from $210.75/mt the prior day — freight easing even as cargo values rose.
  • • West Mediterranean LPG saw butane rise ($31/mt to $614/mt) on naphtha strength while propane traded flat ($573.75/mt) following a competitive CIF NWE large-cargo bid; Rhine levels at Kaub read 24cm, a partial rebound from the sub-20cm reading flagged Aug 5, though still expected to fall marginally in the week ahead per German water authority WSV.
  • • Northwest European butane rose on the rebounding naphtha complex; European biopropane premiums fell $23.25/mt week over week amid a quiet summer-holiday demand lull, though S&P Global Energy Horizons analysts said the Middle East war's impact on global biofuel demand has been "overstated," with only Vietnam and Brazil showing genuine war-related adjustments.
  • • US Gulf Coast propane rose to 66.375¢/gal (Enterprise Mont Belvieu), the highest since end-July, even as VLGC freight rates to Japan began falling (Houston-Chiba down to $255/mt) amid "faltering hopes" for a Hormuz peace deal — the arbitrage incentive to the Far East widened to $62/mt from $42/mt on Jul 28. USGC-to-Japan propane loadings are tracking over 2mn mt month to date, on pace for the highest volume since October 2016 per CAS.

Both the Asian and Middle Eastern LPG desks explicitly tied today's rise to the same $34/mt Brent jump, itself driven by lingering Hormuz supply concerns as the US and Iran remain "far apart" on normal-transit resumption — a genuine, single macro driver reunifying what had been two separately-moving legs of the market.
