A heavy sour crude that used to sail to China is now being coked and cracked on the US Gulf Coast, producing diesel (bullish distillate) and residual fuel (bearish HSFO) simultaneously. This is the structural counterweight to the Busan/Hong Kong physical shortage — and it is the reason the desk is comfortable being bearish the HSFO cargo crack while acknowledging genuine tightness in Asian delivered bunkers.
