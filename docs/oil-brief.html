Overall Market Summary
Yesterday's flagged gap risk was the story, and it resolved in exactly the direction warned — but the second-order effect is more important than the first. Cash Dubai surged $8.24/b to $88.99/b at the Asian close, absorbing two sessions of Atlantic strength in a single print, and its premium to same-month Dubai futures blew out $2.91 to $9.95/b. Five October Dubai partials traded actively at $88.95–$89.00. Asian physical did not merely confirm the rally — it overshot it.

The consequence is that the barrel has decoupled East from West in every single product, and that is now the dominant tradeable structure. Naphtha C+F Japan rose $48.25/mt with a cargo clearing at $819/mt and three fresh buy tenders, while CIF Northwest Europe fell $3.75. The gasoil East–West spread collapsed to −$186.49/mt for balance-August, a $64.79 move in one session. Singapore's 380 CST HSFO cash premium is up nearly 96% week over week to a post-June-12 high, while Med 3.5% fuel oil fell $2.50. Asian gasoline cracks widened while European MTBE dropped $37.50/mt. LPG is backwardated in Asia and in contango in Europe. Directional calls are now much less useful than regional relative-value calls.

The single most bearish new datapoint is OPEC+ supply. The Platts survey puts July output at 34.19mn b/d, up 1.85mn b/d and rising for a second consecutive month, as Hormuz easing let Saudi Arabia (+1.00mn b/d to 8.00), Iraq (+0.70 to 2.60) and Kuwait (+0.55 to 1.60) ramp hard. Libya hit a 12-year high 1.33mn b/d. The group is still 6.3mn b/d below quota and far below February's pre-war 42.89mn b/d — so this is recovery, not surplus — but the direction of travel is unambiguous and it is the second month of it.

The single most bullish new datapoint is Russian refining. S&P Global Energy CERA puts 2.8mn b/d — roughly 40% of total Russian refining capacity — offline as of end-July, with Russian crude runs at 3.9mn b/d in July, the lowest for any month since March 2005. Ukraine has reported 12 refinery attacks in August alone. Russian diesel/gasoil exports have collapsed from 3.3mn mt in March to 0.23mn mt so far in August. In a genuinely striking inversion, India is now shipping gasoline to Russia via transshipments near Egypt using sanctioned tonnage. The physical ARA diesel crack stands at $82.95/b against a June average of $44.75/b.

Jet's short is now confirmed by the market's own language. Platts quotes regional traders directly: "European refineries, especially in the Mediterranean, should be maximizing diesel production now as cracks are higher and the regrade value — the difference between jet fuel and diesel cracks — is negative." The Singapore jet cash differential fell for a fourth consecutive session, the MOC saw offers from BP and Trafigura with no bids and no trades, and the US West Coast jet premium to the Gulf Coast has collapsed to 5.47¢/gal from 35.84¢ in May.

And a new supply flashpoint opened in North Africa. Libya's NOC warned it may declare force majeure at the 120,000 b/d Zawiya refinery — the country's sole working plant — after six drone strikes since Aug 8 destroyed gasoline tank 402-T holding 4.5mn litres and hit an unprocessed naphtha tank. The central bank governor resigned the same week. Libya supplies light sweet crude and gas to Europe and had just reached a 12-year production high.

📋 Tender / OSP / Trade Flow — The ESPO Signal

The most informative flow datapoint this cycle is Chinese buying of October ESPO Blend. At least 30 of the roughly 42 cargoes in the October programme had been sold as of Aug 11 — more than two-thirds locked in well ahead of the normal mid-August cycle start, split evenly between one state refiner and one private refiner, and nearly double their combined 17 cargoes for July. Pricing moved from a ~$3/b discount to ICE Brent DES Shandong for September cargoes to parity for October, with remaining offers reported as high as a $2/b premium. "Only a dozen cargoes are left for the teapots and Indian buyers," a Shandong trader said.

The stated reason matters as much as the volume: a state-refinery source said Russian Far East barrels were competitively priced and "their arrivals were considered more reliable than those from the Middle East, given the disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab Strait." Comparable-quality Brazilian, Angolan or Middle Eastern barrels would land in China above a $4/b premium to Dated Brent, versus discounts in the $1s/b for delivered ESPO. Iranian Light firmed to a ~$3/b discount from $4/b. Chokepoint risk is now being priced directly into Asian crude procurement, and it is pulling barrels away from the Gulf.

Elsewhere: ADNOC issued its eighth tender since the war began, offering Upper Zakum, Das Blend and Umm Lulu for October–November, closing Aug 13. South Korea will begin importing Argentine crude in 2027 after trial cargoes — Medanito FOB has rebounded to a $2/b discount to the LatAm Brent strip from a three-and-a-half-year low of −$7.60/b on Jul 9, with Taiwan's CPC taking its first cargo. Five Kimanis cargoes and three Ichthys condensate cargoes surfaced for October loading.
