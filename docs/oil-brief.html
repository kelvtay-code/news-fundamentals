🇨🇳 China will not restock at these prices
DEMAND
  • "China is unlikely to replenish its crude stocks unless oil prices and freight costs fall sharply", two crude procurement managers and a crude trader at Chinese state-run companies, plus an executive at a state think tank, told Platts on 16 October.
  • The subheads read "No reserves without sharp price drop", "Backwardation deters strategic buying plans", "Imports rise as depleted commercial stocks cut additional supplies" and "December arrivals expected to drop on costs".
  • "The market is in backwardation", the sources said, which deters strategic buying. Improving imports over September to November would slow China's destocking, while inflows are likely to fall in December because of high prices.
  • Logistics uncertainty has made crude arrivals uneven.
💡 This is the fourth bearish demand item in four sessions, after the OPEC cut on 13 October, the IEA demand cut on 14 October and the IEA release update on 15 October. Crude futures rose through all four. The buyer of last resort has now said in print that it will not buy, and the physical premium is collapsing at the same time.
