How to read this

A ticker appears only if both slopes are positive — least-squares gradient through its IV Implied and GEX Scale series across the window. Slope measures persistence, not just net change: a steady climb scores high, a last-day jump does not.

GEX FLIP marks a crossing from negative to positive gamma — a genuine dealer regime change, worth more than drift inside positive territory. FLAT IV and IV FADING mark the false positives: a positive slope that ends where it started, or below it. Those qualify on the gamma leg alone.

Phase decides the meaning. Rising IV in Mark-up with gamma building is a premium build on a healthy tape. The same numbers in Mark-down are fear repricing — the opposite trade. Grade encodes this; sort order does not.

▲ / ▼ next to the phase is the bullish read: ▲ is Mark-up or Accumulation (expansion into a constructive tape), ▼ is anything else — most often Mark-down, where the same rising IV and gamma build is fear being repriced rather than a premium build.
