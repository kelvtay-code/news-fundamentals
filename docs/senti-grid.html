Read this before trading off the grid

1. This screen measures headlines, nothing else. No Wyckoff phase, no GEX, no IV rank. A 9 here means the news flow is unambiguously positive — it does not mean dealer positioning or structure agrees. Run it through voltbuster and riskcheck before it becomes a trade.

2. Semis are split, not uniformly strong. AMAT crushed estimates and still fell ("earnings weren't quite good enough"), ASML picked up a "pricey after a 193% run" note, and TSM is called 35% overvalued — all three dropped out of this cut. The AI trade is being priced selectively.

3. Two names here are crowded shorts. Michael Burry is short MU and NBIS, both of which score 7 on rallying headlines. A stock can be rallying on news and be a crowded short at the same time; the senti score measures only the former.

4. Read the caveat line, not just the number. Insider selling, premium valuations and overvaluation flags sit inside several bullish scores and are printed inline where they exist.
